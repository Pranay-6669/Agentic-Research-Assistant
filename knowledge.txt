ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE RESEARCH KNOWLEDGE BASE


ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Artificial Intelligence is a branch of computer science
focused on developing systems capable of performing tasks
that normally require human intelligence.

AI applications include natural language processing,
computer vision, robotics, recommendation systems and
intelligent assistants.


GENERATIVE AI

Generative AI refers to artificial intelligence systems
capable of generating new content such as text, images,
audio, video or software code.

Large Language Models are widely used in generative AI
applications for understanding and generating natural language.


LARGE LANGUAGE MODELS

Large Language Models are neural-network-based models trained
on large amounts of textual data.

They can perform tasks such as question answering,
summarization, text generation and information extraction.


RAG

RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

RAG combines information retrieval with Large Language Models.

The system retrieves relevant information from an external
knowledge source and gives that information to the LLM as
context before generating an answer.


AGENTIC RAG

Agentic RAG extends traditional RAG by introducing reasoning
and decision-making.

An Agentic RAG system can evaluate retrieved information and
decide whether additional retrieval, another data source or
another tool is required before generating the final answer.


AI AGENTS

An AI agent is a system that can use a language model to
reason about a task and interact with tools or external
systems.

Tools may include APIs, databases, search engines,
calculators and retrieval systems.


VECTOR DATABASES

Vector databases store numerical embeddings representing
documents or other information.

They support semantic similarity search and are widely used
in Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems.


CHROMADB

ChromaDB is a vector database that can store embeddings,
documents and metadata.

It supports similarity search and persistent local storage.


EMBEDDINGS

Embeddings are numerical vector representations that capture
semantic information.

Text with similar meaning tends to produce vectors located
closer together in vector space.


CREWAI

CrewAI is a framework for building applications involving
multiple AI agents.

Agents can be assigned roles, goals and tasks and can work
together as a crew to complete more complex workflows.
